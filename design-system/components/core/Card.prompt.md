Panel container with optional header (title + right action); outline (white) or tile (gray) variants. Inset is the inner hairline box.

~~~jsx
<Card title="Recent activity" action="View all tasks">…</Card>
<Card variant="tile" title="Upcoming triggers">…</Card>
~~~

Nest, don't shadow: a Card holds Insets/rows with their own hairline. Cards are flat — no drop shadow.
